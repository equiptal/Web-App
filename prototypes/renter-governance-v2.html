Four detectors need a field the platform does not store

Everything above runs on data already in your account. These four do not

  • Who raised and who awarded no actor stored on a request or an award, so no finding can name a person
  • Shared with one firm, then won by that firm shares are written but there is no endpoint to read them back
  • A requirement dropped after bids landed the post-bid edit is capped at one but never recorded
  • A deadline extended to let a late bid in only the current deadline is stored, never its history

Every figure comes from your own requests, bids, link submissions, supplier records, deal rooms and awards. Prices are compared per cycle through the same calculation the quotation uses, with Fridays excluded. A flag is a prompt to look, not a finding of wrongdoing.
