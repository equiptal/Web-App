What the platform cannot answer yet

Everything above runs on data already in your account. These do not, and each names the one thing missing

  • Blocked by one missing field
  • Who raised, who awarded, who approved a deviation no actor on a request or an award
  • Who the request was actually shared with shares are written, never read back
  • What changed in a post-bid edit the fact is stored, the diff is not
  • A deadline extended after bids landed only the current value is kept
  • Who viewed competitor bid values before close no access log exists
  • Case state, justification text, outcome nowhere to store a workflow
  • Blocked by a whole missing subject
  • Conflict of interest and related parties no employee records, no owner identity, no declarations
  • Delivery reality: serial, plate, telematics, gate nothing records what physically arrived
  • Equipment on site before its request existed arrival is only recorded if somebody marks it, and almost nobody does
  • Invoices, IBAN, payment diversion the platform handles no money
  • Approval thresholds and splitting there is no approval step at all
  • Rate card adherence, framework agreements no rate cards exist
  • Debarment and sanctions screening no external list integration

Every figure comes from your own requests, bids, link submissions, supplier records, deal rooms and awards. Prices are compared per cycle through the same calculation the quotation uses, with Fridays excluded. A flag is a prompt to ask, not a finding of wrongdoing, and every case carries a one-press request for the buyer's explanation.
