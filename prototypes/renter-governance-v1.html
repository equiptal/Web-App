Blocked on data the platform does not hold

Each line is a real control, held back by one missing field or one missing capture flow

  • Who raised and who awarded no actor stored on a request or an award
  • Segregation of duties needs that same actor before it can be detected
  • Approval queue and cycle time there is no approval step in the product
  • Change log per request edits are capped but never recorded
  • Last login and dormant accounts not tracked
  • Serial and plate per unit never captured on the renter side
  • Named operator per machine a request states operator terms, not a person
  • Operator iqama, licence and medical no operator record to attach them to
  • Certificate expiry dates equipment papers arrive with no expiry field
  • Site permits and lifting plans no upload surface
  • SLA actuals response time is a declared term, never a measurement
  • Incidents and pre start checks needs a driver facing flow
  • Gate check in and out the scan that turns paperwork into a site fact
  • Muqeem, GOSI, Qiwa, ZATCA, Fahas, Najm no integration yet

Every figure on this page comes from your own awards, requests, projects and supplier records. Billable days exclude Fridays, which this platform does not bill, using the same calculation as the quotation. Exposure is what has accrued to today, not a forecast.
