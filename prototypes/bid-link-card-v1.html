Rules this layout holds to

ruleA row appears only when the request carries it. Never “Fuel: —”. An empty row teaches a supplier to skim the block, and then he skims the row that mattered.

compressThe image pairs what the card separates. Mob and demob share a column, FAT and fuel share a column — four rows do not fit at 28px, and two labelled pairs do. The card keeps them apart because it has the room.

person_addThe fifth row makes every share an invitation. A supplier bidding through the form is off platform by definition, and this is the moment he is most likely to join — he is already working.

link_offIn the image the app line is text, not a link. Nothing in a picture is clickable, so the same offer is a real link in the card below it.

visibility_offEverything else stays out. Hours, nationality, certificates, night shift, overtime, payment terms — the bid form asks each one structurally and gets an answer back as data instead of prose.

translateArabic mirrors; numbers do not. The layout flips, and the code, the dates and the count stay left-to-right.
