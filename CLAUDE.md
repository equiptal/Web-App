## Change log

**It lives in [`docs/CHANGELOG.md`](docs/CHANGELOG.md), newest first. Write new entries THERE,
never here.**

Read it before changing a surface you have not touched: nearly every entry records a trap, a
reversal, or a reason an odd-looking line is load-bearing, and re-deriving those from `git log`
costs more than reading them.

⚠️ **This file is re-read into the system prompt on EVERY turn; the archive is not.** The log
lived here until 2026-09-22 and had reached **537 KB**, then **81 KB** four days after a split that
left the ten newest behind - about 20,000 tokens paid on every question in every session, whatever
it was about. It regrew because the rule pointed at this file. Keep this file to RULES: anything a
reader needs on a task they have not chosen yet.

⚠️ **Both files are CRLF on disk, and `sed` on this machine STRIPS the CR** (logged
2026-09-22, after it silently rewrote 531 KB to LF). `head -n` / `tail -n +` preserve it.
